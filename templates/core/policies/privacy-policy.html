1. Introduction

Valor Liquor Store ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and services.

2. Age Verification

Legal Compliance Requirements

Due to the nature of our business, we are required by law to verify that all users are of legal drinking age. We collect and verify age-related information through:

  • Government-issued ID verification during account creation
  • Age verification upon delivery

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Personal Information

  • Name and contact information
  • Date of birth
  • Billing and shipping addresses
  • Payment information
  • Purchase history

3.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Shopping behavior and preferences
  • Browser and device information
  • Website usage statistics

4. How We Use Your Information

4.1 Order Processing

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Verify your age and identity
  • Send order confirmations and updates
  • Process payments and prevent fraud

4.2 Customer Service

  • Provide customer support
  • Respond to inquiries and requests
  • Process returns and refunds
  • Handle complaints and feedback

4.3 Marketing

  • Send promotional emails (with consent)
  • Personalize your shopping experience
  • Analyze shopping patterns
  • Improve our services

4.4 Legal Compliance

  • Verify legal drinking age
  • Comply with regulations
  • Prevent underage sales
  • Maintain required records

5. Information Sharing

We maintain strict control over shared information and only share with trusted partners essential for our services.

5.1 Service Providers

  • Delivery partners for order fulfillment
  • Payment processors for transactions
  • Age verification services
  • Customer support systems

5.2 Legal Requirements

  • Law enforcement when required
  • Regulatory compliance requests
  • Court orders and subpoenas
  • Protection against fraud

6. Data Security

6.1 Security Measures

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Secure payment processing
  • Regular security assessments
  • Access control and authentication

6.2 Data Protection

  • Encrypted data storage
  • Regular backups
  • Employee training on security
  • Incident response planning

7. Your Privacy Rights

Your Rights Include:

  • Access your personal information
  • Request data correction or deletion
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

8. Children's Privacy

Age Restriction Notice

Our services are strictly for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect or maintain information from persons under 18 years of age.

Our Commitment:

  • Strict age verification at registration
  • No marketing to underage individuals
  • Immediate deletion of underage user data
  • Cooperation with parents/guardians

9. Updates to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect:

  • Changes in our practices
  • Legal requirements
  • New features and services
  • User feedback and concerns

We'll notify you of any material changes to this policy.
