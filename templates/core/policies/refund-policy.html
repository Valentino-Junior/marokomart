Return & Refund Policy

Important Notice: Due to the nature of our products and legal requirements, we have strict policies regarding returns and refunds. Please read carefully.

1. General Policy

For safety and legal reasons, we cannot accept returns of opened alcoholic beverages. Our return policy is strictly limited to specific circumstances outlined below.

2. Eligible Returns

Returns are only accepted in the following cases:

  • Damaged products (must be reported within 24 hours of delivery)
  • Incorrect items delivered
  • Unopened products with manufacturing defects
  • Orders cancelled before shipping

3. Non-Returnable Items

  • Opened bottles or containers
  • Products without original seals intact
  • Special order items
  • Gift cards/coupons

4. Return Process

4.1 Damaged or Incorrect Items

  1. Contact customer service within 24 hours of delivery
  2. Provide order number and photos of damaged items
  3. Wait for return authorization
  4. Package items securely in original packaging

4.2 Cancellations

  • Orders can be cancelled within 1 hour of placement
  • Cancellation fees may apply after shipping
  • Contact customer service immediately for cancellations

5. Refund Information

5.1 Refund Methods

  • Original payment method (for eligible returns)
  • Store credit (preferred method)
  • No cash refunds available

5.2 Refund Timeline

  • Processing time: 3-5 business days
  • Credit card refunds: 5-10 business days
  • Store credit: Immediate upon approval

5.3 Additional Fees

  • Restocking fee: 10% for unopened returns
  • Shipping costs are non-refundable
  • Return shipping costs are customer responsibility

6. Quality Guarantee

Our Quality Promise

We stand behind the quality of our products. If you receive a defective product:

  • Document the issue with photos
  • Contact us within 24 hours
  • Keep the original packaging
  • Do not open the product

100% Satisfaction Guaranteed

Legal Notice: All returns must comply with local and federal laws regarding alcohol transportation. We reserve the right to refuse any return that violates applicable laws or our policies.
